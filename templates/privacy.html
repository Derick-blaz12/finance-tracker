Privacy and your data

This is a learning project and a demo, not a commercial service. Please use made-up or low-sensitivity data, and never enter account numbers, card details or anything you couldn't tolerate losing.

What is stored

  • Your email address (lowercased).
  • A salted hash of your password. The password itself is never stored.
  • Your transactions: date, type, description, category and amount.
  • Recent failed login attempts (email and IP address), deleted after 15 minutes.

Who can see it

Other users cannot see your data. The person running the app can technically read the database, including your transactions, because they are not encrypted inside it. Passwords cannot be read, only verified.

Your control

  • Export: download all your transactions as CSV from the Account page.
  • Delete: permanently remove your account and every transaction from the Account page.

Backups

If backups are made, they may keep a copy of deleted data until they are replaced. Backups are kept only by the person running the app.

If the project is shut down

This is a hobby project with no guarantees. If it is stopped, export your data first. Do not rely on it as your only record.
